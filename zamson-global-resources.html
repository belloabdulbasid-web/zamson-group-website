About Zamson Global Resources Limited

Zamson Global Resources Limited is a Nigerian multidisciplinary technical services company incorporated in 2003. The company provides specialised services including civil engineering, electrical engineering, procurement, construction and related technical solutions.

Our approach combines established technology, innovation and practical expertise to support projects from planning and development through construction and plant upgrades.

We are committed to delivering cost-effective solutions, maintaining service quality and building lasting relationships with our clients.
